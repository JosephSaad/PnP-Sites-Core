Accepted paste format & validation rules

Group questions under a [Category: Name] header. Each question may declare [Type: Single|Multi|TrueFalse] (auto-detected when omitted).

[Category: Mathematics]
[Type: Single]
1. What is the value of x if 2x + 5 = 15?
Image: https://example.com/photo.png
A) 3
B) 5
C) 10
D) 7
Correct: B
Rationale: Subtract 5, then divide by 2.
  • Rule 1: every letter in Correct: must exist in the options.
  • Rule 2: Multi answers are comma-separated (e.g. A, C) and need 2+ keys.
  • Rule 3: every question must sit under a declared [Category].
  • True/False questions can omit options — True/False are generated automatically.
